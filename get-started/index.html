The verified customer journey

How onboarding will work when released

  1. Create a secure account

    Use the single RCBID + FUND identity system; the corporate website does not create accounts or collect passwords.

  2. Build the business profile

    Confirm legal/business name, service regions, industries, capabilities and objectives. Record what is confirmed versus unknown.

  3. Choose your offer

    Select Scan, FIND or PURSUE directly. The Scan is optional; annual or monthly billing is selected before checkout.

  4. Review payment terms

    Secure Stripe checkout verifies the buyer and links payment to the correct account, company and order.

  5. Receive the paid outcome

    Scan: an automated source-linked report with checks for freshness, fit and evidence, delivered privately only if payment and safety gates pass. Subscription: verified entitlements to advertised features.

  6. Manage the account

    Access invoices and service status, corrections and support. Subscribers can manage payment methods, cancellation and recovery.

Subscription disclosure: FIND/PURSUE are designed for a 7-day trial with a card on file. After the trial, the selected CAD monthly or annual price renews automatically unless cancelled. Trial, renewal, cancellation, refund and customer-portal handling must pass provider-backed tests before these subscriptions can be offered.

Privacy: Do not email payment-card details, passwords, government ID numbers, confidential financial records or protected tender documents. Company documents belong in the secure customer portal once verified. An email enquiry is not a purchase or an account.
