VEPKAR — Airflow Energy Recovery

Studies potential conversion of existing HVAC/exhaust airflow into electrical power.

What engineering evidence is required?

The first engineering gate is instrumented measurement of electrical power, airflow, added pressure drop, fan/system energy penalty, noise, safety and repeatable 72-hour operation. Re-entry is a qualified paid feasibility or pilot-design assessment only after commercial and technical scope is agreed; no production generator is offered. An airflow turbine cannot create energy without changing the energy balance.

No measured power generation, commercial return, patent grant or working production prototype is claimed on this page. Sensitive design and unfiled invention material remains private.
