How the customer will receive value after release

  1. Purchase and identity: provider-confirmed CAD $47 payment or admitted FIND/PURSUE subscription links to a unique customer and secure company profile.
  2. Automated intake: customer enters company capabilities, service region, target opportunities and objectives; the system records an order and source provenance.

  4. Evidence review: each relevant lead shows source, date and an evidence status of CONFIRMED, NEEDS VERIFICATION, POTENTIAL BLOCKER or UNKNOWN. Material uncertainty triggers an automatic evidence hold, correction or refund path rather than fabricated certainty. Routine human review is not part of the future standalone Scan.
  5. Private delivery: a prioritized customer report or subscription workspace is released only after successful quality checks, with customer notification, correction/refund route and delivery trace.

Current state: this is the specified fulfillment contract, not proof that automated intake, billing, notifications, evidence-quality controls or secure delivery are currently available. The original application database, payment webhook and full customer lifecycle remain release-gated.
