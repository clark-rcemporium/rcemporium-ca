Current Product

One platform. Two opportunity engines.

RCBID and FUND use different source data, but they solve one customer job: show the opportunities that could bring money into the business and help decide what deserves time.

RCBID — Earn

Government procurement and tender intelligence

Contracts, tenders, RFPs, RFQs, standing offers and procurement notices, with source links, requirements, fit reasons, gaps, deadlines and next actions.

FUND — Fund

Funding and grant intelligence

Grants, innovation, commercialization, training, equipment, export and R&D support, with eligibility flags, unknowns, blockers, evidence gaps and official sources.
