About RC Emporium

RC Emporium Technologies Inc. is a cleantech hardware startup focused on identifying and commercializing high-impact business opportunities. We combine patent intelligence, government procurement data, and AI-powered analysis to help businesses discover untapped revenue streams.

Based in Winnipeg, Manitoba, we specialize in:

  • Patent-Based Ventures: Identifying expired patents with commercial potential
  • Government Procurement: Discovering government bids and funding opportunities
  • Cleantech Innovation: Developing hardware solutions for energy efficiency and sustainability
  • Business Intelligence: AI-powered analysis and market research
