## Overview

This is the professional corporate headquarters website for RC Emporium Technologies Inc., a cleantech hardware startup focused on government procurement intelligence, patent-based business opportunities, and cleantech innovation.

- **Hero Section:** Company mission and primary CTA (Found Money Scan)
- **About Section:** Company overview and core competencies
- **Ventures Section:** Showcase of active ventures (RCBID & FUND, Patent Venture Engine, VEPKAR)
- **Contact Section:** Contact information and location
- **Footer:** Legal links and copyright
