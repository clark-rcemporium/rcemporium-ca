Current Primary Product

RCBID + FUND

One opportunity-intelligence platform for businesses that need to find relevant government work and legitimate funding support, understand what fits, identify gaps, and decide what deserves action.

Open the current RCBID + FUND surface

RCBID

Government procurement and tender intelligence: contracts, tenders, RFPs, RFQs, standing offers and procurement notices.

FUND

Funding and grant intelligence: grants, innovation, commercialization, training, equipment, export and R&D support.

What the product is designed to do

  1. Create one company and capability profile.
  2. Match relevant contracts and funding opportunities.
  3. Show fit, requirements, gaps, blockers, deadlines and official sources.
  4. Save, dismiss, pursue, task and track outcomes in one pipeline.

Truth model

CONFIRMED
Supported by an authoritative source or verified company information.
NEEDS VERIFICATION
Appears likely, but more evidence is required.
POTENTIAL BLOCKER
A requirement or condition may prevent pursuit.
UNKNOWN
The platform does not currently have enough evidence.

Important: RCBID + FUND does not guarantee a contract, funding, eligibility, bid compliance or application success. Published opportunity values are opportunity values, not expected revenue.
